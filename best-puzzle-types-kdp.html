Not all puzzle books sell equally on Amazon KDP. Some formats have massive demand and low competition. Others are oversaturated or require too much effort to create profitably. Here is the honest breakdown of every major puzzle type — and which ones are worth your time.

Word Search — The Reliable Workhorse

Word search is the most popular puzzle type on Amazon KDP by volume. It appeals to the widest audience — kids, seniors, hobbyists, and everyone in between. The search volume for word search books is consistently high year-round, with spikes around holidays.

The key advantage of word search is how easy it is to niche down. A generic word search book competes with thousands of titles. A "Large Print Dog Breed Word Search for Seniors" competes with maybe a dozen. Same puzzle type, completely different competitive landscape.

Sudoku — High Demand, Easy to Scale

Sudoku is the second most popular puzzle type on KDP. It has a dedicated audience that buys consistently — particularly seniors and commuters. The demand is less seasonal than word search, which means steadier sales year-round.

The best-selling sudoku books on KDP differentiate by difficulty level and print size. Large print easy sudoku for beginners and large print hard sudoku for experts are both strong niches with loyal buyers. A book that clearly communicates its difficulty level in the title outperforms generic sudoku collections.

Crossword — High Value, Higher Effort

Crossword books command higher prices on Amazon — typically $8.99 to $12.99 — because buyers perceive them as more premium. A well-made crossword book can outsell a word search book on a per-unit basis simply because of the price premium.

The challenge is creation. Quality crossword puzzles require that every clue and answer be accurate and appropriate for your audience. A crossword generator like PuzzleForge handles the grid construction, but your clues need to be relevant and correct. This extra quality control step is what keeps competition lower than word search.

Word Scramble — Underrated Niche

Word scramble books are consistently underestimated by KDP publishers. The demand is real — particularly for kids' activity books and educational markets — but far fewer publishers target this format. That means less competition for the same buyers.

Word scramble books also work well in combination with other formats. A "Word Search and Word Scramble Activity Book" offers more value and justifies a higher price than either format alone.

Mazes — Kids Market Gold

Maze books dominate the kids' activity book category on Amazon. Parents and grandparents buy them constantly for road trips, rainy days, and school break activities. The audience is huge, the repeat purchase rate is high, and the competition — while growing — remains manageable with strong niche targeting.

The best-performing maze books on KDP are themed — dinosaur mazes, princess mazes, space mazes — and targeted at specific age ranges. A "100 Easy Dinosaur Mazes for Kids Ages 4-8" is far more searchable than a generic maze book.

The Verdict

If you are just starting out, word search is the best entry point. It has the highest demand, the most niche opportunities, and the easiest creation workflow. Once you have published a few word search titles and understand the KDP process, add sudoku and maze books to your portfolio.

Crossword books are worth doing if you are willing to invest in quality clues — the higher price point and lower competition make them excellent earners for publishers who do it right.
